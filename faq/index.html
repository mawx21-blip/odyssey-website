Pricing

What things cost.

Transparent pricing, no sales tricks.

Starting at $50 per cut for standard residential lots. The final price depends on:

  • Lot size (square footage of lawn)
  • Terrain (slopes, obstacles, anything that slows us down)
  • Any additional add-ons you request

Send us your address and we'll send a firm quote within 2 business hours.

Biweekly usually costs more per visit because the grass is taller and takes longer to cut. Weekly service gives you the best per-visit price and the healthiest-looking lawn — which is why we recommend weekly from March through October.

We'll send you one clear price up front — before any work starts — so you know exactly what the job will cost. No surprises, no "we'll figure it out when we're done" pricing.
