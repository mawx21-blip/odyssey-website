Careers at Odyssey Lawn Care

Build a career with a lawn care company that takes pride in the work.

Odyssey Lawn Care is a Southaven-based lawn care company serving homeowners across DeSoto County. We're looking for dependable, hard-working team members who want steady work, clear expectations, and a company that takes professionalism seriously.

Why Work With Odyssey?

A professional team with standards.

We work across Southaven, Olive Branch, Horn Lake, and DeSoto County with systems built around quality, accountability, and teamwork.

Clear expectations

You know the standard, the schedule, and what good work looks like before the day starts.

Professional equipment and systems

Local Southaven-based company

Quality-focused work

Who We're Looking For

Dependable people who care about doing the job right.

Odyssey is a good fit for people who want structure, take pride in physical work, and understand that professionalism matters even when nobody is watching.

  • Show up on time
  • Can work outside in Mississippi heat
  • Take pride in clean, detailed work
  • Can follow systems and instructions
  • Communicate well
  • Respect customers and their property
  • Want to be part of a growing company
Open Roles

Roles we hire for as the company grows.

Even if we are not actively hiring for every role, we are always open to hearing from dependable people who may be a good fit.

Lawn Care Team Member

Entry-level and experienced team members who help with mowing, trimming, blowing, cleanups, mulch, and detail work on residential properties.

Crew Lead / Experienced Lawn Care Technician

Experienced lawn care professionals who can help lead routes, maintain quality, communicate clearly, and keep the team moving efficiently.

Seasonal Christmas Light Installer

Seasonal team members who can work carefully, follow safety standards, and help install Christmas lights during the busy holiday season.

Standard field start time is 7:00 AM Monday-Friday. Saturday work may be required when weather causes delays.
Requirements

What applicants should be ready for.

We keep the expectations clear because the work depends on consistency, safety, and trust.

  • Reliable transportation to the shop/job start point
  • Ability to work Monday-Friday starting at 7:00 AM
  • Willingness to work outdoors
  • Ability to follow safety and quality standards
  • Valid driver's license preferred, required for lead/driver roles
  • Lawn care experience preferred but not required for entry-level team members

FAQ

Questions about working here.

Lawn care experience is preferred, especially for crew lead or experienced technician roles, but it is not required for entry-level team members. Dependability, work ethic, and willingness to learn matter most.

Standard field start time is 7:00 AM Monday through Friday. Saturday work may be required when weather causes delays.

Odyssey Lawn Care hires for steady lawn care roles and seasonal work, including Christmas light installation. Available roles can vary by season and route needs.

A valid driver's license is preferred for all applicants and required for lead or driver roles.

People who show up on time, work professionally, follow systems, communicate clearly, respect customers' property, and take pride in quality work tend to do well here.
