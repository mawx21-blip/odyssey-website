Why Homeowners Book This Service

Overgrown bushes, back in shape.

Shrubs and hedges look incredible when they're shaped — and terrible when they're not. Most homeowners don't have the tools, time, or know-how to do it right, and DIY trimming can permanently damage a plant if you cut at the wrong time or in the wrong spot.

We shape your shrubs and hedges the right way for each species — keeping them healthy, symmetrical, and looking sharp. And we haul away every clipping. You come home to clean beds, not piles of yard waste.

  • Proper technique. We trim at the right height, angle, and time of year for each plant type.
  • Complete cleanup. All clippings bagged and hauled away — no debris left behind.
  • Scheduled or on-demand. Book a one-time trim or add it to your seasonal rotation.
  • Full-service crew. Same team that handles your mowing, if you combine services.

It depends on the plant. Most landscape shrubs benefit from 1–2 trims per year. Fast-growing hedges (like privet or holly) may need 3 times a year to stay sharp. We'll tell you what we recommend for your specific plants.

For most shrubs in DeSoto County, late winter or early spring (before new growth) and mid-summer (after first flush) are ideal. Flowering shrubs have specific timing — we'll let you know if we recommend waiting until after bloom.
