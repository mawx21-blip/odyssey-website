Why Homeowners Book This Service

Fresh mulch, done in one visit.

A fresh layer of mulch instantly transforms your beds — it locks in moisture, regulates soil temperature, suppresses weeds, and makes your whole property look sharper. But only if it's installed properly.

We deliver the mulch, spread it to the right depth, and leave your beds with a clean, consistent finish. You get one price up front, no upcharges at the end.

  • Delivered and installed. Mulch brought to your property, spread evenly, and raked to a clean finish.
  • Proper depth. 2–3 inches — deep enough to work, not so deep it smothers plants.
  • Clean edges. Mulch stays in the beds, off the lawn and walkways.
  • One clear price. You see the total before we start — no surprise charges at the end.

About our mulch installation service.

We work with hardwood mulch as our default — it breaks down well in DeSoto County soil and looks clean for most landscapes. Cedar, cypress, or dyed colors (black, brown, red) are available on request — let us know what you prefer when you get your quote.

Spring is ideal — usually March through May. A fresh layer going into the growing season helps suppress weeds and lock in moisture right when your plants need it most. Fall installs also work well if you want beds to look sharp heading into winter.

Depends on your bed square footage and how deep we're going. At 2–3 inches deep, a cubic yard of mulch covers about 100–120 square feet. We'll measure your beds and size the job appropriately — you don't have to figure this out.
